# Product context

## Product

ITC Field Source Manager is a conversation intelligence workflow for in-person sales teams. Field sales representatives visit shopkeepers and capture sales conversations. Field Source Managers need reliable evidence about visit outcomes, retailer needs, recurring objections, and follow-up commitments.

## Users

- Field sales representatives using a phone in a shop or on a route, including when connectivity is unreliable.
- Field Source Managers reviewing team and territory activity, conversation patterns, and follow-up actions.

## Product purpose

Preserve field conversations reliably, then turn consented recordings into reviewable, evidence-backed sales signals that help managers understand demand and support next actions.

## Product principles

- Recording must work without a live network after the PWA has been installed and opened once.
- Every reported insight must be traceable to a conversation and, where available, an exact transcript segment.
- Separate inferred opportunity from confirmed order data.
- Present confidence and analysis status; do not imply certainty where the system has none.
- Show demo fixtures as synthetic and distinguish them from newly captured conversations.
- Use conversation evidence to support coaching; do not create opaque employee rankings or automated employment decisions.
- Treat recording consent, access control, retention, deletion, and auditability as product requirements.
