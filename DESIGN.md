# Design direction

## Source

Use the Impeccable design skill as the project design process. It is a design critique and refinement workflow, not a fixed visual theme. Keep product and design decisions in this document as the system evolves.

## Typography

Use Hanken Grotesk throughout the product, bundled locally so the interface remains legible after the app shell has been cached offline.

## Visual direction

- Both experiences are Operate surfaces: clarity, scanability, and dependable state feedback come before decoration.
- The manager experience is a high-signal operations workspace. Establish hierarchy through typography, spacing, and restrained color instead of stacking cards inside cards.
- The field capture experience is mobile-first and task-focused. Recording, pause/resume, stop, history, and sync status must remain immediately understandable.
- Use chart color consistently by meaning and keep labels, legends, and evidence readable at phone and desktop sizes.
- Provide a non-chart data view for each important report and support keyboard navigation, visible focus, sufficient contrast, and reduced motion.

The current app-shell CSS is scaffolding only. Record the final surface compositions and tokens after the Impeccable-guided design phase, against rendered screens.

## Reporting language

Use plain language for findings. Label inferred insights, show confidence and transcript evidence, and distinguish recorded facts from model suggestions.
